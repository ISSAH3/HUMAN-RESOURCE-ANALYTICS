# HR Analytics Dashboard using SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Human Resources data using **SQL** for data exploration and **Power BI** for interactive dashboard development.

The objective is to help HR managers and business leaders understand workforce demographics, employee attrition, compensation, and employee performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard answers important HR business questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments experience the highest employee turnover?
- Which job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does overtime affect employee attrition?
- How are salaries distributed across departments and job roles?
- What are the workforce demographics?
- How do employee satisfaction and performance compare?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- Research & Development has the highest employee population and the highest attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians recorded the highest employee turnover.
- Employees working overtime have a significantly higher attrition rate than those who do not.
- Managers receive the highest average monthly salary.
- Sales Representatives receive the lowest average monthly salary.
- Higher education levels are associated with higher average salaries.
- The majority of employees fall within the **26–35** age group.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Improve employee retention in the Research & Development department.
- Review overtime policies to reduce employee burnout.
- Strengthen career development and promotion opportunities.
- Continue investing in employee training and development.
- Monitor employee satisfaction regularly through HR engagement initiatives.
